## 🔑 FLORIDA MMJ FAST FACTS (for content reference)

- **State fee:** $75/year — paid to Florida OMMU, not the provider
- **Card valid:** 1 year (state card)
- **Physician cert valid:** 210 days (~7 months) — must renew separately
- **Both must be active** to legally purchase at dispensaries
- **Physical card arrives:** ~10 business days after OMMU approval
- **Same-day dispensary access:** Yes — once physician submits to MMUR
- **Active cardholders:** 840,000+
- **Dispensaries statewide:** 630+
- **Qualifying conditions include:** Chronic pain, PTSD, anxiety (as comorbid), cancer, epilepsy, Crohn's, glaucoma, HIV/AIDS, MS, Parkinson's, ALS, terminal conditions
