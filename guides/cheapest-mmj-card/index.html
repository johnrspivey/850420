The Process
How to Get Your Florida MMJ Card
01
Book an in-person eval with a licensed Florida physician
Florida law requires your first evaluation to be face-to-face. Choose a provider above, pick a location near you, and schedule online. Most initial visits take 10–15 minutes. Bring proof of FL residency — a Florida driver's license or state ID works, or a utility bill, lease agreement, or government mail showing your FL address if your ID doesn't match. Bring any relevant medical records if you have them — not always required but speeds things up.
02
Get entered into the Florida Medical Marijuana Use Registry (MMUR)
Once your physician approves you, they submit your certification to the state MMUR. You can legally visit a dispensary the same day — you don't need to wait for the physical card.
03
Complete the state application and pay the $75 fee
You'll get an email with login credentials to the MMUR portal. Upload proof of FL residency and pay the $75 annual state fee. Physical card arrives by mail in about 10 business days after approval.
04
Renew every 210 days via telehealth
Your physician certification expires every 210 days (~7 months). After your first in-person visit, all renewals can be done via telehealth from home. Your state card renews annually. Both must be active to buy at dispensaries.

Do You Qualify?
Florida Qualifying Conditions

These conditions are explicitly listed under Florida Statute 381.986. A physician may also certify conditions of the same kind or class. Only a licensed FL physician can determine if you qualify.

What does "as comorbid" mean? Conditions like anxiety and depression don't independently qualify on their own in Florida — but if you have a qualifying condition (like PTSD or chronic pain) and also experience anxiety or depression alongside it, your physician may factor those in. Your doctor makes the call based on your full clinical picture.
