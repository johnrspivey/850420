Florida's Two-Part Renewal System

This is the most common source of confusion for Florida MMJ patients. Your card and your physician certification are two separate things — both must stay active simultaneously for you to legally purchase at a dispensary.

Part 1 — Every 210 Days
Physician Recertification
Your OMMU-registered physician must recertify you every 210 days (approximately 7 months). This visit confirms you still qualify and updates your certification in the state registry. In-person required by Florida law. If you have already seen the physician before, telehealth may be available — confirm with your provider.
$85–$169
Part 2 — Every Year
State Card Renewal
Your physical OMMU card expires annually. Renewal is done entirely online through the Florida Medical Marijuana Use Registry. Pay the $75 state fee, confirm your information, and your renewed card arrives by mail in 10–15 business days.
$75 state fee
⚠ Critical: If your physician certification lapses — even one day — you cannot purchase from any Florida dispensary until you complete a new in-person visit and your doctor re-enters your certification in the registry. Your physical card being valid does not matter. Both must be active simultaneously.

Step-by-Step Renewal Process

  • Set a reminder at 180 days
    Do not wait until your certification is about to expire. Schedule your renewal appointment at day 180 — that gives you a 30-day buffer for scheduling delays. Most providers send reminders but do not rely on them. Put it in your calendar the day you get certified.
  • Book your physician renewal visit
    Contact your current provider or choose a new one. If you are switching physicians, the new doctor must conduct an in-person evaluation at your first visit with them — after that you may qualify for telehealth renewals. Bring your current MMJ card and any relevant medical records.
  • Complete the in-person visit
    Your physician reviews your qualifying condition and updates your certification in the OMMU registry. Same-day registry submission is standard at most providers — you can visit a dispensary the same day your doctor submits. Bring your Florida ID and your current card.
  • Renew your state card online
    Log into the Florida Medical Marijuana Use Registry at mmuregistry.flhealth.gov. Complete the annual card renewal form and pay the $75 state fee. Your renewed physical card arrives in 10–15 business days. You can continue using your existing card while the new one processes.
  • Collect your dispensary renewal discount
    Most major Florida dispensaries offer a discount on your renewal day — typically $75 off a $150+ purchase. Show your renewal receipt at the counter. Trulieve, Curaleaf, MÜV, AYR, and Fluent all participate. That discount essentially covers your state fee in product.

What to Know Before You Renew

⚖️ Florida Law: In-person physician evaluation is required for your first visit with any provider. Subsequent renewals may qualify for telehealth if conducted with the same physician. Florida Statutes Chapter 381.986 governs all MMJ certifications. Telehealth-only certification is not permitted.

Can I switch providers at renewal?

Yes, but your new provider must conduct an in-person evaluation at your first visit with them — even if you have been a patient elsewhere for years. After that initial in-person visit with the new provider, subsequent renewals with them may qualify for telehealth. Factor this in if you are switching to save money.

What if I miss my 210-day window?

If your physician certification expires, your dispensary access is immediately cut off — even if your physical OMMU card is still valid. You must complete a new in-person evaluation and wait for your doctor to re-enter your certification in the state registry before you can purchase again. There is no grace period.

Can I renew if I am out of state?

The state card renewal (the $75 OMMU fee) can be completed online from anywhere. The physician evaluation, however, must be completed in person with a Florida-based OMMU-registered physician. If you are a seasonal resident or travel frequently, schedule your renewal visit before you leave Florida or plan a return trip within your 210-day window.

Lost or expired card — replacement process

If your physical card is lost, complete the OMMU Change, Replacement, or Surrender Request Form at mmuregistry.flhealth.gov. Include a copy of your Florida ID and pay the $15 replacement fee. Replacement cards typically arrive in 2–4 weeks. You cannot use an expired card while waiting for a replacement.

Veteran and low-income fee waivers

Florida waives the $75 state fee for qualified veterans, SSDI recipients, and Medicaid participants. Apply for the waiver through the OMMU registry when completing your renewal. Most physician providers also offer discounted evaluation fees for veterans — ask when you book.

Common Renewal Questions

How much does it cost to renew a Florida MMJ card in 2026?
Minimum $160 — $85 for the physician renewal visit with Ozark MMJ Cards plus the mandatory $75 state OMMU fee. Most patients pay $174–$235 depending on their chosen provider. Veteran and low-income fee waivers are available for the state portion.
Can I renew my Florida MMJ card via telehealth?
It depends on your provider and history. Florida law requires in-person evaluation for your first visit with any physician. If you have previously seen the same physician in person, subsequent renewal evaluations may be conducted via telehealth. Confirm directly with your provider before assuming telehealth is available.
How often do I have to renew?
Physician recertification is required every 210 days — approximately every 7 months. The state OMMU card renewal is annual. These timelines are independent so you will complete more physician visits per year than state card renewals.
When should I start the renewal process?
Start at day 180 — 30 days before your certification expires. This gives you a buffer for scheduling delays. If you wait until your certification is within a week of expiring and your provider is booked out, you risk a gap in access.
Can I go to any dispensary after I renew?
Yes. Once your physician submits your renewed certification to the OMMU registry — which most providers do same day — you can visit any licensed Florida dispensary. You do not need to wait for your new physical card to arrive in the mail.
Does my dispensary offer a renewal discount?
Most major chains do. Trulieve, Curaleaf, MÜV, AYR Wellness, and Fluent all typically offer $75 off a $150+ purchase on your renewal day when you show your renewal receipt. Ask at the counter — it is not always advertised.
