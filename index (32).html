What to Know as a Central Florida Patient

⚖️ Florida Law (HB 387): First evaluation must be in person — no exceptions. Renewals every 210 days can be done via telehealth. Both your physician certification and state card must stay active simultaneously to purchase legally.

Telehealth vs. local clinic — which is better?

For price, statewide telehealth providers win. Ozark at $39 and Leafwell at ~$51 effective are both cheaper than most Orlando/Tampa walk-in clinics. The tradeoff is that you still need one in-person visit for your initial certification — telehealth providers coordinate this through partner networks. If convenience and same-day walk-in availability matter more than squeezing every dollar, Cannabis Card Orlando and MMJ Health both have strong local options.

Can I get certified the same day in Orlando?

Yes. Cannabis Card Orlando guarantees same-day approval with appropriate medical records and a valid Florida ID. MMJ Health also submits to the OMMU registry the same day. You can visit a dispensary immediately after your doctor submits your certification — no need to wait for the physical card.

Are veteran or military discounts available?
Yes. Most major dispensaries in Orlando and Tampa offer 15–20% off for veterans and active duty with valid ID. Some clinics also offer veteran discounts on evaluation fees — ask when you book.
