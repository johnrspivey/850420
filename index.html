How to Get Approved

  1. Confirm you have a qualifying condition Florida covers chronic pain, PTSD, anxiety (as comorbid), cancer, epilepsy, Crohn's, ALS, Parkinson's, HIV, MS, glaucoma, and more. Doctors can also certify conditions "of the same kind or class." Most reputable clinics won't charge you if you don't qualify.
  2. Grab your medical records if you have them Not always required, but prior diagnoses, prescriptions, or test results make approval faster. A year or more of records is ideal. Don't have records? You can still be evaluated — just be ready to describe your condition and symptoms clearly.
  3. Book a clinic — in person for first-timers Florida law requires your initial evaluation to be face-to-face (HB 387). Telehealth is only for renewals. Bring a valid Florida driver's license or state ID as proof of residency — or a utility bill, lease agreement, or government mail showing your FL address if your ID doesn't match.
  4. Complete your evaluation Typically 10–30 minutes. The doctor reviews your condition, and if you qualify, enters your info into the state registry — often the same day. You can visit a dispensary the moment your doctor submits your certification.
  5. Pay the state fee and apply online After your doctor submits your profile, OMMU emails you to complete your application. Upload proof of Florida residency and pay $75–77.25. You can shop at dispensaries immediately — before the physical card arrives.
  6. Receive your card Physical card arrives by mail in about 10 business days after OMMU approval. A digital version works at most dispensaries right away. Both your physician cert and state card must be active to purchase — they expire on different timelines.

Common Questions

Can I get my Florida MMJ card fully online?
No — not for new patients. Florida law (HB 387, effective July 1, 2023) requires an in-person evaluation for your first certification. Only renewals are eligible for telehealth. Anyone advertising a fully online new-patient process is not compliant with state law.
How long does the whole process take?
From booking to shopping at a dispensary: as fast as the same day if your doctor submits immediately. Physical card arrives in about 10 business days after OMMU approval. You don't need to wait for the card — dispensary access starts when your physician enters you into the registry.
Does insurance cover any of this?
No. MMJ evaluations and cannabis products are not covered by any insurance in Florida. All costs are out of pocket.
How often do I have to renew?
Your doctor's recommendation must be renewed every 210 days (about 7 months). Your state card is valid for one year. Both must be active to legally purchase at dispensaries — if either expires, you lose access until it's renewed. Set reminders at 45 days out for both.
What if I don't qualify?
Most reputable clinics — including DocMJ, My Florida Green, and Green Health Docs — offer a full refund if the physician determines you don't qualify. Ozark MMJ Cards does not publicly list a money-back policy. Always confirm refund terms before you book.
Can I use my Florida card in other states?
No. Florida MMJ cards are only valid in Florida. Florida does not participate in any cross-state MMJ recognition agreements. Transporting cannabis across state lines is a federal offense regardless of your card status.
What does "comorbid" mean for anxiety and depression?
Anxiety and depression don't independently qualify in Florida. But if you have a listed qualifying condition — like PTSD or chronic pain — and also experience anxiety or depression alongside it, your physician can factor those in. Your doctor assesses your full clinical picture.
