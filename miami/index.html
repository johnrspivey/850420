South Florida Patient Notes

⚖️ Florida Law: First evaluation must be in person. Renewals every 210 days may be via telehealth with an established physician. Both physician certification and OMMU card must be active simultaneously to purchase legally.

Is Miami more expensive for MMJ than the rest of Florida?

Local in-person clinics in Miami tend to be pricier than North and Central Florida — most local options run $149–$199 for new patients. However, statewide providers like Ozark ($39) and KindHealth ($99) are available to Miami residents and compete strongly on price. Do not assume you need to use a local Miami clinic.

Can I get certified the same day in Miami?

Yes. KindHealth offers same-day approval and immediate OMMU registry submission — you can visit a dispensary the same day. Most local Miami clinics also offer same-day service. Call ahead to confirm appointment availability.

What about Fort Lauderdale and Broward County patients?

All statewide providers serve Broward County. Curaleaf, Trulieve, MÜV, and AYR all have multiple Broward locations. Certified Marijuana Doctors has locations serving the Fort Lauderdale area. KindHealth's North Miami location is also accessible from southern Broward.

Palm Beach County patients

Statewide providers are your best value option. In-person, Trulieve, Curaleaf, and AYR all have West Palm Beach and Boca Raton locations. Telehealth providers like Ozark and Leafwell serve Palm Beach County patients without requiring a local clinic visit after your initial in-person evaluation.

Common Questions

What is the cheapest total cost to get an MMJ card in Miami?
$114 total — $39 with Ozark MMJ Cards plus the $75 state fee. Best local walk-in option is KindHealth at $99, putting your total at $174.
Is KindHealth the cheapest Miami walk-in option?
Yes, at $99 for new patients KindHealth is the most affordable verified local walk-in provider in Miami-Dade. Their North Miami location at NE 79th Street serves most of the Miami metro.
Do I need to use a Miami-based provider?
No. Any Florida-certified OMMU physician can certify you. Statewide providers like Ozark and Leafwell are fully available to Miami-Dade residents and are significantly cheaper than most local in-person clinics.
Are veteran discounts available in Miami?
Yes. Most major dispensaries in South Florida offer 15–20% off for veterans and active duty with valid ID. The state OMMU fee waiver is also available for qualifying veterans — apply through the state registry when completing your card application.
Can a seasonal Florida resident get an MMJ card in Miami?
Yes. Florida MMJ cards are available to seasonal residents who can demonstrate Florida residency — a utility bill, lease agreement, or seasonal residency documentation is typically accepted. You must complete your first evaluation in person while in Florida.
