What to Know as an 850 Patient

⚖️ Florida Law (HB 387): Your first evaluation must be in person — no exceptions. Renewals every 210 days can be done via telehealth from home. Both your physician certification and state card must remain active simultaneously to purchase legally.

No local clinic cheaper than $39?

Correct. The cheapest local walk-in options in the Pensacola and Fort Walton area typically run $100–$175 for new patients. Ozark MMJ Cards at $39 is a statewide provider — you'll drive to an in-person appointment once, then handle every renewal by phone or video from home.

Can I use a Pensacola dispensary the same day?

Yes — as soon as your doctor submits your certification to the OMMU registry, you can walk into any licensed dispensary in Florida. Most clinics submit the same day. Call ahead and confirm their submission timeline before you book your appointment.

Military and veteran discounts

Northwest Florida has a heavy military population. Most major dispensary chains — Trulieve, Curaleaf, MÜV — offer 15–20% off for active duty and veterans with valid ID. Ask at the counter on your first visit.

Common Questions

Is there a dispensary in Destin or Crestview?
Destin and Crestview are served by dispensaries in the Fort Walton Beach and Niceville corridor. Trulieve, MÜV, and Fluent all have locations within a short drive of both cities. Check each chain's store locator for the nearest location.
Do I have to go to a Pensacola clinic specifically?
No. Any Florida-certified MMJ physician can certify you regardless of where they're located. You can book with Ozark MMJ Cards or another statewide provider and see a doctor anywhere in Florida for your in-person visit.
How long does it take to get certified in the 850?
Same-day dispensary access is possible if your doctor submits to the OMMU registry on the day of your appointment. The physical card arrives by mail about 10 business days after OMMU approval. You don't need to wait for the card to shop.
What's the cheapest total cost for a Pensacola patient?
$114 — $39 doctor fee with Ozark MMJ Cards plus the $75–77.25 mandatory state fee paid to the Florida Department of Health. Every other provider charges more for the evaluation.
