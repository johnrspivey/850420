Common Questions

Are transfer fees different from new patient fees?
Yes. A transfer is when you're already a registered Florida MMJ patient with an active card but want to switch to a new physician. Transfer fees are typically lower than new patient evals. Ozark charges $39 for transfers — same as new patients. My Florida Green charges $149 for transfers vs $169 for new patients.
Can I use my Florida card in other states?
No. Florida MMJ cards are only valid in Florida. Florida does not participate in any cross-state MMJ recognition agreements. Transporting cannabis across state lines is a federal offense regardless of your card status.
What does a money-back guarantee actually cover?
It covers the doctor's evaluation fee if the physician determines you don't qualify for a Florida MMJ card. It does not cover the state fee (you haven't paid it yet at that point). Always read the guarantee terms on the specific provider's website before booking.
Are any of these providers available in Pensacola / Northwest Florida?
Ozark MMJ Cards has locations statewide — confirm their Northwest Florida locations on their website when you book. My Florida Green and DocMJ also have statewide coverage. Leafwell operates via telehealth after your initial in-person visit, which can be at any certified FL physician.
