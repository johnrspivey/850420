Buyer's Guide

Why Dry Herb vs. Carts?

Florida dispensaries sell both flower and cartridges. Carts are convenient but more expensive per gram. A dry herb vaporizer works with the flower your dispensary sells — more medicine per dollar, and you control exactly what goes in it.

Temperature Matters

Lower temps (325-365F) preserve terpenes and produce lighter, more flavorful vapor — better for daytime. Higher temps (375-430F) extract more THC and produce thicker vapor — better for evening and pain relief.

Florida-Specific Considerations

  • Humidity — ceramic chambers handle FL humidity better than metal ones
  • Heat — look for devices that perform well in warm ambient temperatures
  • Discretion — if you are a public patient, odor resistance and compact size matter
  • Durability — if you are a daily patient, build quality pays off over time
